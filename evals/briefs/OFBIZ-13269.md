# OFBIZ-13269 — Need to have a support for maximum discount cap in Promotions

**Tipo:** Task · **Prioridad:** Minor · **Componentes:** order

## Descripción

*Scenario:*

While configuring a Cart Range Percent Discount for promotions, there doesn’t seem to be an out-of-the-box option to set a maximum discount cap. For instance, if the business wants to offer a 15% discount on the cart total, but with a maximum discount limit of $100 irrespective of the cart value, the system currently applies the percentage across the total cart value without a ceiling.

The primary aim is not to enforce a minimum margin rule for the product price but to set a maximum discount limit regardless of the cart total particularly for the high valued cart totals. 

The requirement also considers a case whereby system is applying single cart promotion at a time just to simplify the scenario.

*Solution Proposal:*
Extending the ProductPromoAction entity with maxDiscountAmount field and considering this configuration in the promotion engine to validate the max discount that can be provided to the customer.
